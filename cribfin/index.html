The problem

You shouldn't have to rely on "trust me, it's fine."

Managing property from a distance usually means grainy WhatsApp photos from a relative, rent that arrives late (or not at all), and finding out about problems months after they started.

Cribfin replaces guesswork with evidence: timestamped photos, video walkthroughs, verified tenants, and rent tracked to the naira.
